For each I brainstormed what people actually do to share and learn (e.g. post photos, ask for technique feedback, swap product recommendations) rather than jsut listing random app features.


### Simulating group work environment.


This assignment assumes a group setting, but I'm working alone. To still get the benefit of critique and divergence, I treated my tutor consultation as a stand-in for a group meeting. 

I did not have a certain interest topic area, but I was drawn to rock climbing after my tutor mentioned it. Mainly because the idea had room for a lot of gamification and achievements baords and that sounded fun.

The more I reflected on this, I realised that leaderboards dont address a real pain point. It's a surface-level motivation and  doesn't change how climbers actually improve. 

### Brainstorming to explore gaps in existing community spaces. 

I first looked at modes of discussion outside of the traditional  text response replies on online forums. Looking at physical in person discussions gives opportunity to incorporate these set behaviours and environments into the digital platform. 

 Brainstorming revealed possible areas:

* debates
* feedback
* critiques
* reflection
* reinterpretations

### Pivoting My Idea: video feedback with timestamps.

> **Problem statement:** *current forum and community platforms lack the features that allow efficient process of giving feedback. There is only the option of typing out text heavy paragraphs and adding text timestamps. *How might we create a more effortless feedback experience that replicates that of in-person process between two people?*

The concepts: 

Users can recieve feedback from others selecting a specific 'climbing move' frame in the video and give drawing annotations and texts. Having feedback tied to specific frames/time stamps meant better organisation and more clarity.
