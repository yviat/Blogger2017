### What is the brief asking of me? What does this app need to do?

For this assignment I knew I didn't jsut want a 'sick' idea, I wanted a concept that could I could stand behind in a portfolio. 


That meant two things:

1. A strong exsiting gap for a certain community 
2. Technically achievable within unit skills

### How I explored the space
I started with a broad brainstorm of possibble communities and activities:

_1. art and crafts
2. sewing 
3. photography
4. rock climbing
5. bouldering
6. social adventure group._

For each I brainstormed what people actually do to share and learn (e.g. post photos, ask for technique feedback, swap product recommendations) rather than jsut listing random app features.

### Narrowing towards a direction

I did not have a certain interest topic area, but I was drawn to rock climbing after my tutor mentioned it. Mainly because the idea had room for a lot of gamification and achievements baords and that sounded fun.

The more I reflected on this, I realised that leaderboards dont address a real pain point. It ass surface-level motivation, but it doesn't change how climbers actually improve. My tutor nudged on this, adn that feedback exposed my tension: my instinct is to over-design and gamify, but my technical skills and the brief push me towards something simpler and more focused.
